Our Story

A family business since 1972

Stan Stebnitz started the company in 1972 doing general carpentry and small additions. His sons bought it in 1986 and his grandson Chris became the third-generation owner in 2008. Today it is the area’s design-build remodeling firm, with a five-year warranty on workmanship.
